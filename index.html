§ 01 —

About

My academic background is in Biological Psychology, where I studied conflict processing with behavioral, electrophysiological, and cognitive modeling approaches. My PhD takes me in a different but complementary direction, investigating negation across multiple modalities.

My clinical training runs alongside the research. It reflects my interest in psychology across the whole spectrum, from the experimental to the clinical.
