About

My background is in Biological Psychology, where I studied conflict processing with behavioral, electrophysiological, and cognitive modeling approaches. My PhD takes me in a different but complementary direction: the cognitive processes behind negation, across multiple modalities.

Alongside research, I’m training as a Cognitive Behavioral Psychotherapist. It reflects my interest in psychology across the whole spectrum, from the experimental to the clinical.
